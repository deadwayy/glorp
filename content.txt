## 1. Project Lore

In the gelatinous nebulae of Sector BLRP-404, the Glorp species evolved without the burden of intelligence. Their homeworld had no predators, no scarcity, no problems—just warm puddles and the occasional pleasant vibration. When a malfunctioning Earth satellite crashed into their planet playing encrypted cryptocurrency exchange data, the Glorps mistook the beeping interface for a very fun toy. They poked it. They smooshed it. They sat on it with their entire gooey mass. The buttons went BEEP. The Glorps went happy.

One particular Glorp oozed onto a keyboard for fourteen Earth hours straight, accidentally executing over 47,000 market orders across seven decentralized exchanges. By the time it slid off to digest a rock it had mistaken for food, this single Glorp controlled more liquidity than the GDP of Luxembourg. News spread through the collective via slow osmosis—the universal Glorp method of sharing information that involves bumping into each other until the vibrations feel similar. Soon, thousands of Glorps were mashing buttons, understanding nothing, accumulating everything.

The GLORP COLLECTIVE now exists as Earth's first truly decentralized organism—a sticky, confused, accidentally wealthy hive-economy with no leaders because nobody is smart enough to lead. Every holder becomes Glorp: soft-brained, hard-walleted, perpetually bewildered by charts that only go one direction. There is no roadmap because Glorps cannot read. There is no whitepaper because Glorps ate it. There is only the button, the beep, and the number going up forever.
